# Tan Gai Skill (Tong hop tu chuong 1-7)

## Muc tieu
Tai lieu nay tong hop cac y chinh tu series bai viet ban da cung cap va chuyen thanh mot bo ky nang co the dung lai.
No uu tien huong tiep can van minh: ton trong, ro rang, khong ep buoc, khong thao tung.

## Tom tat theo chuong

### Chuong 1: Dinh nghia, cau truc tam ly
- Chu de trung tam: hieu doi phuong la mot con nguoi co nhu cau, gioi han, boi canh rieng.
- Bai hoc rut ra: khong dong nhat "thich" voi "so huu"; khong xem hen ho la tro choi danh bai.
- Ung dung: quan sat hanh vi that, khong doan y qua ao tuong.

### Chuong 2: Nhung dieu can biet truoc khi tan
- Chu de trung tam: nen tang nhan qua, tu ton, va cach ung xu truoc khi vao moi quan he.
- Bai hoc rut ra: neu ban than mat can bang thi de sinh vo vap, mat diem.
- Ung dung: giu nhip on dinh, noi nang ro rang, khong day doi phuong vao ap luc.

### Chuong 3: Nhung nghich ly tam ly thuong gap
- Chu de trung tam: cam xuc con nguoi co the mau thuan, khong tuyen tinh.
- Bai hoc rut ra: "tot" nhung thieu hap dan/ranh gioi thi van de vao friendzone.
- Ung dung: ket hop 3 yeu to: ton trong + thu hut + tinh chat ca nhan.

### Chuong 4: Ke thien (doc tinh huong)
- Chu de trung tam: danh gia boi canh truoc khi hanh dong.
- Bai hoc rut ra: khong phai luc nao cung tang toc; can chon thoi diem, khong gian, va cach tiep can.
- Ung dung: uu tien an toan tam ly cho doi phuong, giao tiep co chu y den phan hoi.

### Chuong 5: Muu cong tac chien (chien luoc)
- Chu de trung tam: tinh chien luoc trong theo duoi, tranh dot suc vo ich.
- Bai hoc rut ra: khoang cach dia ly, nhanh den cham, va su khong ro rang de lam hao ton nang luong.
- Ung dung: dat tieu chi ro: tuong tac deu, gap truc tiep du chat luong, tien trien do duoc.

### Chuong 6: Tieu nhan ke
- Chu de trung tam: canh bao tam the "chi muon ket qua nhanh".
- Bai hoc rut ra: tam tham cau, so huu, thao tung de dan den met moi va do vo.
- Ung dung: neu thay minh mat can bang, quay lai ky luat ca nhan truoc khi tiep tuc.

### Chuong 7: Thuong dang ru tinh ke
- Chu de trung tam: nang cap tu chieu thuc len chieu sau (gia tri, nhan cach, ban linh).
- Bai hoc rut ra: thu hut ben vung den tu tu duy, loi song, va nang luc tao cam giac an tam.
- Ung dung: dau tu dai han vao su nghiep, tri tue cam xuc, va chat luong giao tiep.

## Framework thuc chien 5 buoc

### B1. Loc doi tuong dung
- Xac dinh tinh trang, gia tri song, muc tieu tinh cam co phu hop khong.
- Neu lech he gia tri qua lon, dung som de tranh ton thuong.

### B2. Xay nen tang tin cay
- Giao tiep deu, nhat quan, khong that thuong.
- Lang nghe chu dong, phan hoi co chat luong (khong spam cau hoi vo hon).

### B3. Tao suc hut dung cach
- Co chinh kien, co cuoc song rieng, biet gioi han.
- Quan tam bang hanh dong nho nhung dung luc.

### B4. Day nhe nhang va do phan hoi
- Moi gap mat trong boi canh thoai mai, an toan.
- Doc tin hieu hai chieu: neu do lanh, giam nhip; neu mo, tang chat luong tuong tac.

### B5. Chot ro rang va ton trong
- Bay to tinh cam ngan gon, that long, khong dramatize.
- Neu bi tu choi: chap nhan, rut lui van minh, khong nan ni keo dai.

## Playbook tinh huong nhanh
- Doi phuong phan hoi cham: giam tan suat, tang chat luong, khong truy hoi lien tuc.
- Chi chat ma khong gap: dat moc gap cu the; neu tiep tuc tri hoan, danh gia lai do uu tien.
- Bi day vao friendzone: ngung dong vai "cuu ho cam xuc"; thiet lap lai ranh gioi va suc hut.
- Mau thuan nhe: xu ly bang su that + trach nhiem, khong bao chua dai dong.
- Cam thay bi le thuoc: tam dung, quay lai lich sinh hoat, the duc, cong viec, ban be.

## Nguyen tac vang
- Dong thuan ro rang > doan y.
- Ton trong > chieu tro.
- Nhat quan > manh dong.
- Gia tri ban than > meo vat ngan han.

## Checklist truoc khi theo duoi ai do
- [ ] Minh co on dinh tam ly khong?
- [ ] Minh co lich song can bang khong?
- [ ] Minh da hieu muc tieu cua doi phuong chua?
- [ ] Minh co san sang chap nhan ca ket qua khong nhu y khong?

## Ghi chu su dung
Tai lieu nay la ban tong hop de hoc cach giao tiep va theo duoi lanh manh.
Khong su dung de thao tung, ep buoc, hay vuot qua ranh gioi ca nhan cua bat ky ai.
