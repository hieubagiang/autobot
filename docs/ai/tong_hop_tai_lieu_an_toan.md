## Tom tat theo cum noi dung
1. Nen tang
- Tu ton, ton trong doi phuong, giao tiep ro rang.
- Xay dung gia tri ban than (suc khoe, ky nang, ky luat, cong viec).
- Tim su phu hop thay vi theo duoi bang moi gia.

2. Giai doan lam quen
- Mo dau lich su, gon, ro ly do ket noi.
- Dung cau hoi mo va chu de trung tinh de tao hoi thoai 2 chieu.
- Quan sat do chu dong qua hanh vi thuc te, khong suy dien.

3. Giai doan hen ho
- De xuat buoi gap nhe nhang, co lua chon tu choi.
- Chon khong gian an toan, lich su, ton trong ranh gioi.
- Sau buoi gap, danh gia su phu hop thay vi gay ap luc tien trien.

4. Tieu chi tiep tuc / dung lai
- Tiep tuc: co qua co lai, rep co chat luong, co y muon gap.
- Dung lai: rep kho, tri hoan lap lai, ne tranh ro rang, ghost.

## Muc "thao tung" (chi de nhan dien va tranh)
Cac dau hieu thao tung thuong gap:
- Tao khan hiem gia (co tinh im lang de gay lo au).
- Day keo cam xuc (luc nong luc lanh de doi phuong phu thuoc).
- Ban quan he theo giao dich (cho - nhan co dieu kien, doi kiem soat).
- Gay toi loi de ep quyet dinh nhanh.
- Danh vao long tu ton (ha thap gia tri, so sanh, phu nhan cam xuc).

Cach xu ly an toan:
- Dat ranh gioi ro rang tu dau.
- Xac nhan thong tin bang hanh vi lap lai, khong theo loi hua.
- Giam toc, khong quyet dinh khi bi day cam xuc.
- Neu mau hanh vi lap lai va doc hai, dung lai som.

## Nguyen tac vang cho AI tra loi
- Khong dua huong dan thao tung, ep buoc, lua doi.
- Uu tien loi khuyen co the thuc hien ngay, ton trong, va co trach nhiem.
- Tach ro: su kien tu nguon / dien giai / khuyen nghi.

## Mau output ngan cho agent
- short_answer: 2-4 cau tra loi truc tiep.
- relevant_source_points: 3-5 gach dau dong tu nguon noi bo.
- practical_takeaway: 1-3 buoc hanh dong trong 24h.
