# Bikip2 Distilled (AI Clean)

## Scope
Tai lieu nay tong hop noi dung tu `docs/ref/bikip2` theo huong su dung lanh manh, ton trong va de truy xuat cho AI.

## 1) Diem co gia tri nen giu
- Ton trong doi phuong la nguyen tac cot loi.
- Tu tin, lich su, biet xin loi khi noi sai.
- Kien nhan va nhat quan trong giao tiep.
- Dat cau hoi mo, tranh dao qua sau vao thong tin ca nhan khi moi quen.
- Nhip nhan tin can linh hoat, khong spam.
- Chuan bi ky cho buoi hen dau va uu tien su thoai mai cua ca hai.
- Neu doi phuong khong nhiet tinh keo dai, nen dung som de ton trong thoi gian cua nhau.

## 2) Diem tranh/ap dung can than trong
- Khong dung mind game (co tinh tao thieu vang, tra loi cham de thao tung).
- Khong nham "chien thuat" voi "chan thanh".
- Khong coi viec tim hieu nhieu nguoi la co dinh dung cho moi boi canh.
- Khong tiep can theo kieu cong nghiep, cung mot khuon mau cho tat ca.
- Khong ap dat suy dien ve gioi tinh hay danh gia de quy ket (tham tien, ham dep, ...).

## 3) Mau hinh thuc te de lam quen lanh manh
1. Mo dau gon, ro ly do ket noi.
2. Goi mo bang chu de trung tinh (so thich, cong viec, hoc tap, sach/phim).
3. Quan sat do tuong tac 2 chieu.
4. Neu hop vibe, de xuat gap nhe nhang voi lua chon de tu choi.
5. Sau buoi gap, danh gia su phu hop thay vi ep tien trien.

## 4) Tin hieu nen tiep tuc / nen dung
### Nen tiep tuc
- Rep deu va co noi dung.
- Co hoi dap mo rong chu de.
- Chu dong dat cau hoi nguoc lai.
- Dong y gap mat voi thai do thoai mai.

### Nen dung
- Rep cho co, mot tu keo dai.
- Lien tuc tri hoan khong de xuat phuong an thay the.
- Bieu hien met moi, tranh ne, hoac ghost ro rang.

## 5) Khung nhan tin goi y (an toan)
- Mo dau: "Chao ban, minh thay ban co [diem chung], neu ban ok minh muon lam quen."
- Goi mo: "Gan day ban dang quan tam dieu gi nhat?"
- Chot lich: "Neu ban ranh, cuoi tuan minh moi ban cafe 45-60p cho de noi chuyen. Khong tien cung khong sao nhe."
- Dung lich su: "Cam on ban da chia se. Minh thay minh va ban chua hop nhip, nen minh xin dung o day. Chuc ban nhieu dieu tot dep."

## 6) Nguyen tac ra quyet dinh
- Tu ton + ton trong > meo vat.
- Su phu hop > su theo duoi bang moi gia.
- Chat luong tuong tac > tan suat.
- Nhin hanh vi thuc te > doan y.

## 7) Ket luan cho AI
Neu can tra loi nguoi dung tu nguon nay, uu tien:
- Trich dan nguyen tac giao tiep lanh manh.
- Loai bo goi y thao tung.
- Dua khuyen nghi ngan, ro, co the lam ngay.
